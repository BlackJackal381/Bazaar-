How the estimates work

Buy orders compete at the highest existing buy bid; sell orders compete at the lowest existing sell ask. Proposed prices improve the relevant order by 0.1 coin. Net unit profit = sell price × (1 − tax) − buy price. Market volume uses the smaller of the two published weekly directional volumes. Estimated coins/hour assumes you capture a chosen share of that volume; actual fills can be much slower, and the displayed volume is not your personal trade count.

Capital-limited potential profit assumes your available coins are invested once and orders fill at the displayed prices. The estimates do not model queue position, price movement, partial fills, or inventory limits. NPC sales may be subject to daily caps. Crafting requires recipe unlocks and can have special ingredients or exceptions; only a curated set of simple recipes is included.
